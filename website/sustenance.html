Sustenance & Economy

Pipes, the manna rite, shops and gold. The unglamorous half, and the half that stops a cure from being possible when it is missing.

Pipes

The smoke cure vector needs a lit pipe with the right herb in it. Nothing else maintains that, and when it is not maintained the failure does not look like a pipe problem — it looks like a missing herb, or a cure that silently never fires.

pipes.lua polls the pipe list, refills what has run out, and lights what has gone out. It reads curing/curelist.lua for which herbs are smoked and have.item() for what is actually carried, so it cannot try to pack a pipe with something that is in the rift. 

pipes            # state: keep-up on or off, and each pipe's herb and puffs
pipes on|off     # the keep-up loop
emset pipes now # check immediately rather than on the next poll
SettingDefaultMeaning
pipes.enabledtrueKeep the pipes filled and lit.
pipes.assignunsetWhich herb goes in which pipe.
pipes.poll300Seconds between checks.

The manna rite

Three commands with real waits between them — perform the rite, get the bowl, drink the bowl. One word because it is something you do several times a day and the sequence is fiddly to type correctly under any distraction at all. 

manna

Each step goes through core/act.lua like everything else, so a step that the game would refuse is held rather than sent and lost. The waits are real waits, not guesses: see docs/game/sustenance.md, "The manna rite".

Shops

Achaea's WARES listing is a wall of text with replica numbers in it. Emunah keeps the last one seen and redraws it grouped by category, with every item clickable. 

emset shop.confirmAbove <gp>   # hold back any single purchase over this
Why there is a spend limit

In a listing that buys by replica number, a click is one keystroke away from a very expensive mistake. shop.confirmAbove holds any purchase above the figure rather than sending it, and says what it held.

The gold ledger is verified against Char.Status rather than assumed from what was sent — a purchase the game refused should not appear as money spent.

Loot

Gold on the floor, picked up by replica number. 

emset loot          # state, and how many piles taken
emset loot on|off
emset loot now      # sweep once regardless of the setting
SettingDefaultMeaning
loot.goldtruePick gold up at all.
loot.aloneOnlytrueOnly when nobody else is in the room.
loot.ownKillsOnlyfalseOnly from kills believed to be ours.
loot.noDenizenstrueDo not stop to loot while something is still alive.
pack.idbackpack452292The one pack: gold goes in, purchases are paid from it, antitheft keeps valuables in it.
pack.capacity50Items the pack holds. A pack inside it counts as one.

Gold used to stall the hunt

A pile that could not yet be lifted — no balance, no equilibrium — held the room so the walker would not move on before it was taken. Correct, until the pile was one that could never be lifted, at which point the hunt stopped forever.

The hold is now bounded: gold is never allowed to stall the loop indefinitely, and gold that is not ours does not create a hold at all. Both are asserted in the test suite.
