Upkeep and economy

The things that keep a character ready: pipes that are always lit, the manna rite in one command, shops you can buy from with a click, gold collected and stowed, and a pack thieves cannot empty.

Pipes

Smoking cures need a lit pipe with the right herb in it. When pipes are not maintained, the failure does not look like a pipe problem; it looks like a cure that never fires. Emunah refills an empty pipe with the herb it holds, relights one that has gone out, and reads PIPELIST when it does not know the state, such as after login or a reload.

The PIPELIST and the refills are hidden from the game text; a relight is shown. Relighting is also switched from the emset defs grid.

emset pipes              # each pipe: lit or out, what it holds, puffs left
emset pipes on|off
emset pipes now          # send PIPELIST now, and show it
SettingDefaultWhat it does
pipes.enabledtrueKeep the pipes filled and lit.
pipes.assignunsetWhich herb goes in which pipe.
pipes.poll0Re-check every pipe this often, in seconds. 0 asks only when the pipes are not known.

The manna rite

emset manna performs the rite, gets the bowl and drinks it, each step as soon as the game allows it. Each step goes through the same gate as everything else, so a step the game would refuse waits instead of being lost.

Shops

WARES listings are made clickable. Under each item, an action line buys one, fills your rift from a tun, or opens a choice of quantities. You can also buy by the item's number directly:

buy 476321               # one
buy 50 476321            # fifty
emset shop.confirmAbove 5000   # ask before any purchase over 5,000 gold

The gold comes out of your pack for the purchase, exactly the price, and anything not spent goes back in. With shop.confirmAbove set, a purchase over that price waits for you to confirm it.

ELIST is redrawn as a table, one row per fluid, so you can see at a glance which vials are running low.

Loot

When the last denizen in the room is down, Emunah sends get gold, which takes every pile in the room, and puts the gold in your pack once it is in your hands. Denizens that are not on the kill list do not hold it up, and nor do your guardian angel, your mount and your pegasus.

emset loot               # on or off, and how much has been picked up
emset loot on|off
emset loot now           # sweep the room now
SettingDefaultWhat it does
loot.goldtruePick gold up at all.
loot.aloneOnlytrueOnly with nobody else in the room.
loot.noDenizenstrueNot while anything on the kill list is still in the room.
loot.ownKillsOnlyfalseOnly gold from your own kills.
loot.holdTyped30 sGold you take out of the pack yourself stays in hand this long before it goes back.
pack.idyour packThe one pack, by its number: gold goes in, purchases are paid from it.

Antitheft

Achaea's thieves strip one defence, selfishness, and pick your pocket in the gap before it is back. Emunah closes that gap from both ends:

  • Selfishness kept up, so a strip is answered at once, and generosity cured early.
  • Valuables packed. Anything named in antitheft.sweep goes back into your pack, and loose gold is put away after a few seconds.
  • An alarm when an item leaves your inventory or pack with no command of yours to explain it, or when selfishness is stripped.
  • An enemy walking in puts everything away at once: someone on your city's enemy list, or someone you declared an enemy. A citizen of an organisation marked hostile does not count.
emset antitheft          # selfishness, the pack, and what is kept in it
emset antitheft on|off
emset antitheft now      # put loose gold and valuables away now

Curatives are never swept into the pack; they have to stay loose to be eaten.
