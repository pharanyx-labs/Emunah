A complete combat system for Achaea, running inside Mudlet.

Emunah cures your afflictions in the client, keeps your defences up, shows every balance as it moves, and hunts an area from end to end. The fight is the only thing left for you to think about.

Install Emunah See what it does

Free and open source under the MIT licence. Needs Mudlet 4.10 or later and an Achaea character.

Four systems, built to work as one.

Curing, the interface, defences and hunting all read the same live picture of your character, and every command they send goes through one gate that knows when the game would refuse it.

Client-side curing

Every affliction cured on the right balance, the moment that balance is back.

Achaea's server-side curing stays off. Emunah reads your afflictions from GMCP, decides which cure each balance should spend, and sends it on the prompt it becomes possible. The rules are the reference system's, and you can see and change every one.

  • Every balance at once. Herb, salve, sip, purgative, smoke, focus, moss and tree each choose their own cure, so a salve never waits behind a herb.
  • Locks cured around, not into. Anorexia, slickness, asthma and paralysis shut whole balances. Emunah cures through the one that is still open instead of sending into a refusal.
  • Healing on its own thresholds. Health and mana sips, irid moss and Perform Hands each engage at their own level, on balances that rarely compete.
  • Illusions ignored. Text reports wait for the prompt, one illusion discards the whole block, and a gained affliction is dropped unless the server confirms it.

Live interface

Every balance, affliction and defence on screen, as the game reports it.

The panels draw from the same state the curing engine acts on, after each packet and only where something changed. What the screen shows is what Emunah is about to do.

  • The balance strip. Each balance ready, in flight, recovering with the seconds left, or shut by an affliction. It applies the queue's own rules, so it never shows a balance as ready that Emunah would not use.
  • Vitals that show the trend. Health, mana, endurance and willpower, each with its change since the last prompt.
  • Everything around the text. The room and its people, chat in tabs, the map, your target, and a ticker for anything you must not miss.

Defence maintenance

Choose your defences once. Emunah keeps them up.

Set each defence to keep up, raise once, or leave alone from one clickable grid. Emunah raises what is missing on the balance it costs, and only when curing does not need that balance.

  • Never wasted. A defence that is up is never raised again, a herb defence waits until the herb is in hand, and anything the game keeps refusing stops after three tries with the reason.
  • The right order. Mindseye goes up before blindness and deafness, so you are never left unable to see or hear.
  • Built in, and teachable. Herb, salve, elixir, tattoo and skill defences come with their commands. Anything else is one emset defs add away.
  • The rest of your upkeep. Pipes kept filled and lit, your mount kept with you, and selfishness kept up against thieves.

Automated hunting

Walk an area, clear every room, collect the gold. One key starts it.

emset hunt, or F11, walks the area room by room and fights whatever is on your kill list, while curing and keep-up carry on underneath it.

It stops itself at its health floor, and when mana, endurance or willpower run low. It will not attack with another player in the room; it moves on instead. After a health stop it resumes once you have recovered, and F12 stops everything and retreats three rooms the way you came.

How the hunt runs
  1. ArriveThe walker steps to the nearest room it has not visited, using Mudlet's own map. No mapper script is needed.
  2. Choose a targetThe next denizen on the area's kill list, by its replica number, never by name.
  3. AttackOn the balance your attack needs. As a Priest, penitence and the rite of desolation go first when they pay for themselves.
  4. CollectAfter the last kill in the room, get gold, and the gold goes into your pack.
  5. Move onThe room is clear, so the walker takes the next one.

And the rest of what a character needs.

Each one is a module you can switch on or off, documented in the client with emhelp.

PvP
Attacks the one player you name and no one else. Tracks the afflictions you have given them and recites Zeal verses to stack more.
People and names
Everyone you have seen, with their city, house and class, filled from WHO lists, HONOURS and the Achaea API. Allies and enemies are coloured in the game text.
Presence alerts
An optional warning window when your guardian angel senses an enemy nearby.
Pipes
Refilled with the herb they hold and relit when they go out, so smoking cures are always ready.
Riding
Your mount ordered to follow you and, if you want, you vaulted back on whenever the balance is idle.
Antitheft
Selfishness kept up, valuables packed, and an alert when something leaves your inventory unexplained.
Shops and gold
WARES listings made clickable, purchases paid from your pack, and a confirmation before anything costly.
The manna rite
Three commands and their waits, done for you as soon as each one is possible.

Installed in three steps.

Emunah runs straight from a git checkout, so updating is one command in the client.

  1. Clone the repository

    Anywhere outside your Mudlet profile directory.

    git clone https://github.com/pharanyx-labs/Emunah.git ~/src/Emunah

  2. Install the package

    In Mudlet, open the Package Manager and install Emunah.xml from the checkout.

  3. Point it at the checkout

    Once per profile. Emunah loads, and emhelp shows every module.

    lua EMUNAH_ROOT = "/home/you/src/Emunah"; EmunahBootstrap()

Nothing acts until you switch it on.

Start with emset curing on, add your defences, and hunt when you are ready.
