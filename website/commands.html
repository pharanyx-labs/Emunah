Commands

Everything Emunah does, one module at a time: the same text emhelp shows in the client, generated from the same table.

Using it

emhelp                        # every module, and whether each is on
emhelp curing                 # one module: its commands and every setting
emset                         # status
emset curing on               # a command
emset curing.method minerals  # a setting

There is one prefix, emset, and every setting can be set by name with emset <setting> <value>. In the client, emhelp <module> lists each setting with its current value, and clicking one changes it. The few words outside emset are listed under the module they belong to.

System

Emunah runs off GMCP: vitals, afflictions, defences, inventory and the room arrive from the game and everything else reacts to them once per prompt. Every command goes through one gate that knows when the game would refuse it (stunned, asleep, unconscious, dead, off balance, prone, paralysed, entangled) and holds it rather than wasting it. Death pauses everything until you are alive again. Settings are saved per profile.

emset

status: curing, defences, safety floors, what is tracked

emset <setting> [value]

show or change any setting, e.g. emset curing.method minerals

emhelp [module]

this help: the modules, or one module's commands and settings

emset reload

update from GitHub's main (unless system.update is off), then reload Emunah from disk, keeping its state

emreload

the same, and still works if the command module failed to load

emset debug

debug log: every command sent, and every command held with the reason

emset debug gmcp

trace every GMCP message in and out

emset debug queue|timers|handlers

dump the action queue, the timers or the event handlers

emset skills

your skillsets and ranks, from GMCP

emset skills dump [skillset ...]

write every ability's AB text, from GMCP, to emunah-abilities.txt in the profile

emset queue

what is waiting on each balance, Emunah's and yours

emset queue bal|eq|eqbal|free <command>

send <command> when that balance is free, ahead of everything automated

emset queue clear

drop the commands you queued that have not gone yet

Curing

Tracks your afflictions from GMCP (and from EmunahTriggers.xml, with the reference system's anti-illusion) and cures them on the right balance: herbs, salves, elixirs, moss, smoking, FOCUS, the tree tattoo, COMPOSE, CONCENTRATE, CLOT and WRITHE. One cure per balance at a time, never inside a balance, never while something blocks it (anorexia, slickness, asthma, paralysis...). Follows the reference system's rules for what to cure first and when not to. Sips health and mana, eats moss, keeps cures pulled from the rift into your inventory, and wakes you from a sleep you did not choose.

emset curing

on or off, and every affliction being tracked

emset curing on|off

switch curing on or off

emset pause

pause or resume curing and defences together; after QUIT, resume everything

pp

the same as emset pause: one toggle for curing and defence keep-up

emset keep

the herbs kept in hand, and how many of each

emset keep <herb> <number|off|default>

keep more, fewer or none of a herb in hand; IR shows every herb with a toggle

emset prio <affliction> <balance> <rank>

cure an affliction sooner or later on one balance; lower is sooner

emset ownprios [on|off]

use your own ranks and situational rules from conf/priorities.conf and conf/situations.conf, or go back to the shipped ones

sleep

RELAX INSOMNIA if it is up, then SLEEP, marked as yours: Emunah neither wakes you nor raises insomnia until you are up

Defences

Raises the defences you choose and keeps them up. Each one is off, defup (raise it once) or keepup (raise it whenever it drops). Knows the command for the herb, salve, elixir and skill defences; tattoos and anything else can be taught. Never raises a defence that is already up, and never spends the balance while curing needs it.

emset defs

the defence grid: click to cycle off / defup / keepup, and pipe relight on / off

emset defs on|off

switch defence keep-up on or off

emset defs add <name> [command]

keep a defence up, with the command that raises it if it is not known

emset defs remove <name>

stop keeping a defence up

emset defs mode <name> defup|keepup|off

raise it once, keep it up, or leave it alone

SettingDefaultMeaning
shield.watchtrueA notice on the chyron while no shield is wielded. Settles on the next inventory change. WIELDED is shown at login either way.
defences.enabledfalse
defences.keepup{}name -> "defup" or "keepup". Written by the defence grid.
defences.commands{}name -> the command that raises it, for defences we ship no command for.

Pipes

Keeps your pipes filled and lit so smoking cures are always ready: refills an empty pipe with the herb it holds, relights one that has gone out, and reads PIPELIST now and then to stay honest. PIPELIST and refills are hidden; a relight is shown. Also toggled from the emset defs grid.

emset pipes

each pipe: lit or out, what it holds, puffs left

emset pipes on|off

switch pipe upkeep on or off

emset pipes now

send PIPELIST now, and show it

pipes.enabledtrue
pipes.assign(unset)Which herb goes in which pipe.
pipes.poll0 sRe-check every pipe this often with PIPELIST. 0 (the default) asks only when the pipes are not known, e.g. after login or a reload.

Riding

Keeps your mount with you: whenever it is in the room and you are not riding it, it is ordered to follow you (free). With keep-up on, you vault back onto it whenever you are off it, but only when the balance is idle: never while anything needs curing, never while bashing or PvP is running, never while it is not in the room. (Not knowing whether you are already riding is no bar: the game refuses a vault onto a horse you are on for free.) While it is not with you, the end of your prompt says so. Also toggled from the emset defs grid.

emset riding

the mount: here or not, ridden or following, keep-up on or off

emset riding on|off

switch riding keep-up (vaulting back on) on or off

Hunting

Bashing and walking. Bashing attacks the denizens in the room, one at a time by replica number, on the balance and equilibrium your attack uses. The walker explores an area room by room. hunt runs both: clear a room, move on. Support mode follows a leader instead: it attacks what they attack and nothing else. Stops itself when health drops below its floor, when a player arrives, or when damage comes in too fast.

emset hunt [off]

walk and bash together; off stops both. F11 toggles it

emset hunt retreat

stop both, go back three rooms the way you came, and stay there

emset support [<name>|off]

emset bash

emset bash on|off

the same as emset hunt / emset hunt off: bash, and walk the area

emset bash attack <command>

PvP

Attacks the player you name, and only that player: it never picks a target by itself. Nothing offensive is sent while it is off. Every free balance (prayer, balance, equilibrium) sends what the mode calls for (F5 attack, F6 offence, F7 hinder, F8 finish), choosing by the afflictions the game has confirmed on them. Holds balance and equilibrium while you are locked, and stops below its health floor.

emset pvp

start or stop attacking the target. F1 toggles it

emset pvp target <name>|off

emset pvp mode attack|offence|hinder|finish

enter a PvP mode and turn PvP on. F5 attack, F6 offence, F7 hinder, F8 finish

emset pvp attack <command>

Allies

A partner is never a target: adding one marks them an ally. While you fight, their arrivals and departures are said. A partner's message that matches one of allies.calls sets your PvP target to the name it calls. Lines only one class sends name who is attacking you, and as what.

emset ally

emset ally add|remove <name>

add a partner (and mark them an ally), or remove one

Antitheft

Keeps selfishness up (it is on the defences grid, emset defs), cures generosity early, puts named valuables in the pack, and raises an alert when an item leaves your inventory or pack with nothing you sent to explain it, or when selfishness is stripped. Map

Walks you anywhere on the map one confirmed step at a time, through the same checks as every other command, so it waits out stun, sleep, paralysis or a lost balance instead of walking into them. Re-plans when you end up off the route or the game refuses an exit; double-clicking a room on the map walks there too. Swims, opens and unlocks doors, goes round a door that will not open, waits out rubble and ferries, and gallops straight runs while mounted. Locator lines (farsee, mindsense, the angel, alertness and the rest) get the room numbers they name as links, and who was where is remembered. The map is the crowd-sourced Achaea map, the same one the IRE Mudlet Mapper uses. Emunah checks for a new version when it loads and every hour, shows what changed, and loads it, waiting for a walk to end first. Rooms you lock or weight are kept outside the map and put back after every update. As you walk, rooms whose name, environment, indoors/outdoors or area disagree with GMCP are corrected. While the mmp package is running it keeps updating the map itself, and Emunah leaves it to it.
