Command Reference

Everything Emunah does, one module at a time — the same text emhelp shows in the client.

Using it

emhelp                        # the modules, and whether each is on
emhelp curing                 # one module: what it does, its commands, its settings
emset                         # status
emset curing on               # a command
emset curing.method minerals  # a setting

There is one prefix, emset. Every setting can be set by name with emset <setting> <value>; in the client, emhelp <module> lists them with their current values, and clicking one changes it. The only words outside emset are emhelp, emreload (which works even if the command module failed to load) and sleep (which is the game's SLEEP, marked as yours so Emunah does not wake you out of it).

System

Emunah runs off GMCP: vitals, afflictions, defences, inventory and the room arrive from the game and everything else reacts to them once per prompt. Every command goes through one gate that knows when the game would refuse it -- stunned, asleep, unconscious, dead, off balance, prone, paralysed, entangled -- and holds it rather than wasting it. Death pauses everything until you are alive again. Settings are saved per profile.

emset

status: curing, defences, safety floors, what is tracked

emset <setting> [value]

show or change any setting, e.g. emset curing.method minerals

emhelp [module]

this help: the modules, or one module's commands and settings

emset reload

reload Emunah from disk, keeping its state

emreload

the same, and still works if the command module failed to load

emset debug

debug log: every command sent, and every command held with the reason

emset debug gmcp

trace every GMCP message in and out

emset debug queue|timers|handlers

dump the action queue, the timers or the event handlers

SettingDefaultMeaning

Curing

Tracks your afflictions from GMCP (and from EmunahTriggers.xml, with the reference system's anti-illusion) and cures them on the right balance: herbs, salves, elixirs, moss, smoking, FOCUS, the tree tattoo, COMPOSE, CONCENTRATE, CLOT and WRITHE. One cure per balance at a time, never inside a balance, never while something blocks it (anorexia, slickness, asthma, paralysis...). Follows the reference system's rules for what to cure first and when not to. Sips health and mana, eats moss, keeps cures pulled from the rift into your inventory, and wakes you from a sleep you did not choose.

emset curing

on or off, and every affliction being tracked

emset curing on|off

switch curing on or off

emset pause

pause or resume curing and defences together

emset prio <affliction> <balance> <rank>

cure an affliction sooner or later on one balance; lower is sooner

sleep

SLEEP, marked as yours so Emunah does not wake you out of it

Defences

Raises the defences you choose and keeps them up. Each one is off, defup (raise it once) or keepup (raise it whenever it drops). Knows the command for the herb, salve, elixir and skill defences; tattoos and anything else can be taught. Never raises a defence that is already up, and never spends the balance while curing needs it.

emset defs

the defence grid: click to cycle off / defup / keepup

emset defs on|off

switch defence keep-up on or off

emset defs add <name> [command]

keep a defence up, with the command that raises it if it is not known

emset defs remove <name>

stop keeping a defence up

emset defs mode <name> defup|keepup|off

raise it once, keep it up, or leave it alone

SettingDefaultMeaning
defences.enabledfalse
defences.keepup{}name -> "defup" or "keepup". Written by the defence grid.
defences.commands{}name -> the command that raises it, for defences we ship no command for.

Pipes

Keeps your pipes filled and lit so smoking cures are always ready: refills an empty pipe with the herb it holds, relights one that has gone out, and reads PIPELIST now and then to stay honest. Its own commands and their output are hidden.

emset pipes

each pipe: lit or out, what it holds, puffs left

emset pipes on|off

switch pipe upkeep on or off

emset pipes now

send PIPELIST now, and show it

SettingDefaultMeaning
pipes.enabledtrue
pipes.assign(unset)Which herb goes in which pipe.
pipes.poll0 sRe-check every pipe this often with PIPELIST. 0 (the default) asks only when the pipes are not known, e.g. after login or a reload.

Manna

Performs the manna rite: the three commands, with the waits between them, done for you once each is possible.

emset manna

perform the manna rite

Hunting

Bashing and walking. Bashing attacks the denizens in the room, one at a time by replica number, on the balance and equilibrium your attack uses. The walker explores an area room by room. hunt runs both: clear a room, move on. Stops itself when health drops below its floor, when a player arrives, or when damage comes in too fast.

emset hunt [off]

walk and bash together; off stops both

emset bash

bashing: target, kills, damage, whether it is running

emset bash on|off

bash the room you are in

emset bash attack <command>

the attack, sent as <command> <replica>

emset walk

the walker: area, rooms left, visited

emset walk start|stop

explore the current area, or stop

PvP

Attacks the player you name, and only that player: it never picks a target by itself. Tracks the afflictions you have given them. Stops below its health floor.

emset pvp

target, their afflictions, attacks sent

emset pvp on|off

start or stop attacking the target

emset pvp target <name>|off

choose the target, or clear it

SettingDefaultMeaning

pvp.attackAtAfflictions2Switch from afflicting to damage at this many stacked.

People

A database of everyone you have seen: city, house, class, and whether they are an ally or an enemy, filled from WHO lists, HONOURS and the Achaea web API. Names are highlighted in the game text by what they are to you.

emset whois <person>

everything known about someone

emset iff <person> ally|enemy|auto

mark someone as an ally or an enemy, or go back to what their city says

SettingDefaultMeaning
