Documentation / Features

Automated hunting

One command walks an area room by room, fights whatever is on your kill list, collects the gold, and moves on when the room is clear. Curing and defence keep-up carry on underneath it the whole time.

emset hunt              # walk and bash together (F11)
emset hunt off          # stop both
emset hunt retreat      # stop, go back three rooms the way you came, and stay (F12)

The loop

  1. ArriveThe walker steps to the nearest room in the area it has not visited yet.
  2. Choose a targetThe next denizen in the room whose kind is on the area's kill list, by replica number.
  3. AttackWhenever the attack's balance allows, with your class's openers first when they are worth it.
  4. Confirm the killThe target leaving the room ends the fight: dead, fled, or taken by someone else all mean the same thing.
  5. CollectWhen the last one is down, get gold. The room is held for a few seconds so the gold is never left behind.
  6. Move onBack to the walker for the next room, until the area is done.

The walker

The walker visits every room in the area, always heading for the nearest one left. It uses Mudlet's own map and speedwalking, so no mapper script is needed, though it uses the IRE mapper's arrival events when that is installed. Candidates are ranked by map distance first and only the closest few are pathfound, so a large area does not stall the client.

While the hunt is running, the walker steps one room at a time and waits to be told to move on. A speedwalk would cross several rooms in one go and walk straight past the denizens in them. On its own, emset walk start explores an area at its own pace.

SettingDefaultWhat it does
walker.stepDelay0.6 sThe pause between steps when the walker paces itself.
walker.avoidnoneRooms the walk never enters.
walker.transitTimeout8 sHow long to keep trying for a room the game will not let you reach, across water or through a closed door, before skipping it.

The kill list

Each area has its own list of which kinds of denizen to fight, saved between sessions. The first time Emunah sees a new kind in an area, it prints a clickable line to add it. Every denizen in the room panel is clickable too, filled when the hunt will attack it and hollow when it will not, and the game's own ih listing becomes clickable in the same way.

Targets are always chosen by replica number, the game's unique number for each creature. Two identical rats in a room are two different targets, and the attack always goes to the one being fought.

The attack

The attack is bashing.attack, sent as <attack> <replica>. It goes when the balance it needs is up, and nothing else of Emunah's is about to spend it. Your target's health comes from the game after every hit, so Emunah knows whether the attack is landing.

For a Priest, the default is Angel Sear, with two openers when they pay for themselves:

  • Penitence brands the target to take more damage from smiting. It is sent only when enough hits are left to make it worth the equilibrium, devotion and mana, and never at a target someone else has already branded.
  • The rite of desolation, on its own cooldown, against denizens only.

A target still standing after bashing.maxAttempts attacks (40 by default) is given up on, so the hunt never grinds forever on something it cannot hurt.

Stopping safely

An unattended hunt has to be able to stop itself. It stops:

  • at its health floor, bashing.stopBelowHealth (50% by default);
  • when health is critical, watch.critical (30%), which stops everything;
  • when mana, endurance, willpower or your class's resource falls below its floor;
  • out of combat, when health drains faster than it should, from bleeding or poison;
  • when the area has been walked, or you move by hand.

Inside a fight, a fast drop in health is not a reason to stop: standing still does not stop a denizen hitting you, so the critical floor is what applies. After a health stop, the hunt resumes on its own once you have recovered ten points above the floor, attacking only and never walking. Any other stop waits for you.

It will not attack with another player in the room; it moves on instead of standing there. bashing.stopOnPlayer ends the hunt outright when someone arrives.

Gold

When the last denizen in the room is down, Emunah sends get gold and puts the gold in your pack. It waits until nothing is left alive and nobody else is in the room, and holds the room for up to four seconds so the walker never leaves gold on the floor. Your guardian angel and your mount do not count as anything alive. See Loot.

Seeing what it decided

emset bash shows the target, the kills, damage dealt and taken, and whether it is running. emset debug prints every command sent and every command held with the reason, so a hunt that is deliberately waiting never looks like one that has hung.

emset bash                     # target, kills, damage, running or not
emset bash attack angel sear   # the attack
emset walk                     # the area, rooms left, rooms visited
emset bashing.stopBelowHealth 60

Classes

Offence sits behind a small class interface: the hunt asks the class whether it can attack, tells it to attack, and reads its resources for the HUD. The class is detected when you log in. Priest is implemented today; see the roadmap for what comes next.
