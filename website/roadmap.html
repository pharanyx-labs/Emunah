Roadmap

Curing, defences, the interface and hunting are complete and in daily use. What comes next is offence, and more classes.

Where it stands

AreaState
Curing, healing and the riftComplete, against denizens and players
Defence keep-up, pipes, riding and antitheftComplete
InterfaceComplete
Hunting: walker, kill lists, lootComplete
People, names and presence alertsComplete
OffencePriest only, one attack with its openers
PvPA named target, opponent affliction tracking, and Zeal verses

Planned

Offensive sequencing

Choosing the next attack by what the opponent already has: a sequencing table shaped like the cure table, driven by the opponent's tracked afflictions.

A second class

Monk, which needs no change to the class interface. A second class is also the only way to prove that sequencing is not quietly Priest-specific, so a guide to adding classes follows it.

Breaking shields

A Priest has no general way to break a shield, so today a shielded denizen is attacked until the attempt limit gives up on it. Breaking the shield waits on a confirmed mechanic.

Judging a denizen before attacking

Using a denizen's constitution to decide whether it is worth fighting, and whether the health floor should be higher for it, once what the figures mean in play is confirmed.

How things get built

These are why the roadmap is shorter than it could be.

  • A wrong assertion is worse than a gap. A missing pattern does nothing. A wrong one asserts an affliction you do not have, and a balance is spent curing it. Where the exact game text or mechanic is unknown, the feature waits.
  • Evidence over reasoning. Mechanics come from timestamped transcripts and GMCP traces, recorded in the repository alongside the code that relies on them.
  • Every fix carries its test. Each behavioural fix comes with a test that would have caught the original report, and nothing is committed with the suite failing.

Something missing, or something wrong? Open an issue, ideally with an emset debug log or a transcript of what happened.
