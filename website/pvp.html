Documentation / Features

PvP

PvP attacks the one player you name, and nobody else. It never picks a target by itself, because in a fight against a person, attacking the wrong one is worse than attacking no one.

emset pvp target sarapis     # choose the target
emset pvp on                 # start attacking
emset pvp                    # target, their afflictions, attacks sent
emset pvp off
emset pvp target off         # clear the target

Only the player you name

The game's list of who is in a room leaves out anyone shrouded, hidden or phased. A loop built on that list would lose an opponent the moment they concealed, or pick the wrong person outright. So a target is a name you typed, and nothing else ever sets one.

Killing your target clears it and ends the session. Your own death ends it too.

Afflictions before damage

Against a player who heals well, raw damage loses the race. So the attack is held until the target is carrying enough afflictions to be finished: pvp.attackAtAfflictions, two by default. The game does not report another player's health, so the afflictions you have given them are the signal there is.

As a Priest with Zeal, Emunah recites Condemnation and Guilt at the target whenever prayer allows. Prayer is its own balance, so verses go out alongside the attack, not instead of it. pvp.verses turns them off.

Tracking your opponent

GMCP reports only your own afflictions. Your opponent's are read from the game text: the third-person lines anyone in the room sees when an affliction shows on someone, such as their face contorting or their body staggering. Emunah reads only those fixed lines and never guesses an affliction from the move that might have caused it. Nothing confirms when an opponent cures, so each entry ages out on a timer.

Safety

PvP stops below pvp.stopBelowHealth, 60% by default, higher than the hunting floor on purpose. Starting PvP stops a hunt, and starting a hunt stops PvP: a player and a denizen cannot share one balance.
