18.10.13 Phials and Vials
(See also HELP REMEDIES, HELP TOXICOLOGY, HELP FILLING)

Vials and phials are small containers, usually for liquids, often with a
stopper or some way in which it may be closed.

Vials hold salves, elixirs, venoms, and the like. Vials hold 200 sips.

Phials hold the various compounds crafted by an Alchemist skilled in
Formulation.

Vial contents can be consolidated with the CONSOLIDATE <fluid> command, which
will pour all of your elixirs into the fewest number of vials. CONSOLIDATE VIALS
will consolidate all fluids in all of your vials at once.

You can POUR from or into a vial:
   POUR [# of sips] <container|fluid> IN|INTO <container|RIFT>

It is also possible to FILL a vial from the rift or a tun:
   FILL <vial> WITH <fluid> FROM <tun|RIFT>

(See also HELP REMEDIES, HELP TOXICOLOGY, HELP FILLING)
