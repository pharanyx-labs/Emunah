5.11 BREATHING
While generally you can move around Achaea very quickly (if you know your
way), some afflictions may cause you to quickly run out of breath, and even
lose health, if you move about too quickly. When you see messages telling you
that you are breathing lightly, breathing heavily, or so on, you will know
that you've been afflicted with one of these afflictions, like asthma.
