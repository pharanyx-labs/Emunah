5.3 ENDURANCE
Endurance represents how worn down you are by constant activity. It goes up
naturally, and goes down as you move or perform other physical actions. When
you run out of endurance, you'll be unable to perform many taxing physical
actions.

The rate at which your endurance recovers is determined by race and the level
of your miniskill called Fitness.
