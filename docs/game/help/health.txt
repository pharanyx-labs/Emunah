5.1 HEALTH
Health (sometimes also called HP), represents your physical well-being. When
your health (which you can see by typing SCORE or by putting PROMPT STATS on -
see HELP PROMPT) reaches 0, you will die (unless some special abilities or
protections comes into play).

Your maximum health may be increased:
- by rising in level (this caps out around level 80, until you reach level 99 and attain dragon),
- by various magical means, and
- by acquiring certain artefacts,
- by completing certain quests

Higher maximum health is always better than lower!

Some abilities used to combat other adventurers cause damage partly based on
maximum health. This means that with higher maximum health you should expect an
increase in some damage received from other adventurers, though the increase is
not as great as the amount by which your own health rose. In any case, you will
definitely be able to withstand more attacks before dying, which is the
important thing.

Read about how to recover lost health in HELP RECOVERY.
