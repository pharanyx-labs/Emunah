5.2 MANA

Mana represents mental strength. When you are out of mana, you will not be able
to perform most actions or abilities that require mental strength.

Learn about how to recover mana in HELP RECOVERY.

Maximum mana increases as you rise in level and by virtue of various other
influences which are rumoured to exist in the realms.
