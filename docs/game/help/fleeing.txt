13.10.1 Fleeing combat
If you are in combat and you are in trouble, how do you get away or flee?
Simple - walk away (or ride away, or fly away, etc).

To flee, simply move in whatever manner you wish.

This does not guarantee that you will be safe, since some opponents, denizen or otherwise, may continue to hunt you. If so, you'll have to learn to run fast and
far!
