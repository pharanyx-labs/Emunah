Fragility (Zeal)                              Known: Yes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            RECITE FRAGILITY <target>
Works on/against:  Adventurers
Cooldown:          1.60 seconds of prayer balance
Details:
Direct this holy verse at a profane enemy of Creation and the next blow
you deal with your mace shall deal greatly increased damage to the
chosen limb(*).

* This increases the next mace attack's limb damage you deal by
approximately 30%.
