Rejection (Zeal)                              Known: Yes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            RECITE REJECTION <target> <direction>
Cooldown:          2.80 seconds of prayer balance
Details:
The verse of rejection is a manifestation of your defiance in the face
of the enemies of Creation. Recite this verse, and even from a far you
shall strike down your foes.

* Does damage to a target in line of sight. Notably, this bypasses
magical shields and prismatic barriers.
