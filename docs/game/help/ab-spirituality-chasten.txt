Chasten (Spirituality)                        Known: Yes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            CHASTEN <target> [BODY|MIND|<affliction>]
Works on/against:  Adventurers
Cooldown:          2.80 seconds of equilibrium
Resource:          100 mana
Details:
The successful believer requires many methods to discourage the chaotic
natures of many of Achaea's inhabitants. Chasten will allow you to
disrupt an adventurer's mental processes, while focusing it upon the
body will disrupt their physical processes.

This ability can be performed off balance if following a smite attack.

Available body afflictions: paralysis, healthleech, clumsiness, nausea,
and asthma.

Available mind afflictions: epilepsy, dizziness, confusion, and
recklessness.

Should your target have all of the afflictions from one pool, they will
receive either addiction (for body) or impatience (for mind).
