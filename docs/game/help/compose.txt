13.7.4 Composing and Concentrating
(Also see: HELP EQUILIBRIUM, HELP HEALINGLIST, HELP AFFLICTIONS,
           HELP BASECOMMANDS)

At times, you may find yourself in a state of panic where you cannot help
rushing about from room to room! If this happens to you, COMPOSE.

At other times, your mental equilibrium may be so disrupted somehow that it
simply will not come back. This can happen, for example, if you are very
cold. Normally when you lose equilibrium, you merely have to wait to
regain it. However, in this disrupted state, equilibrium will not return
no matter how long you wait. If this happens to you, simply CONCENTRATE.

There is an affliction called confusion which will prevent you from being
able to concentrate until you cure the confusion. You can look up what
cures confusion in HELP AFFLICTIONS.

If you came to this help scroll in error, and you are actually looking
for information on how to COMPOSE a personal background or history, see
HELP BACKGROUND.
