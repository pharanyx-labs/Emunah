4.3 THE DEFENCES COMMAND
(See also: HELP LIFE ENHANCING AURA)

Typing DEFENCES or DEF will show you a list of what beneficial things are
affecting you. Generally there will be one line listed for each defence or
effect. DEFENCES BRIEF will show you a less verbose list for those defences
which can be shortened to a single word.

For a list of defences that may be obtained via herbal and alchemical means,
see HELP DEFENCE.

(See also: HELP LIFE ENHANCING AURA)
