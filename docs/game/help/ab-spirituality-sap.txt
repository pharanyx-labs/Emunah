Sap (Spirituality)                            Known: Yes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            ANGEL SAP <target>
Works on/against:  Adventurers
Cooldown:          2.75 seconds of equilibrium
Resource:          5 Angel power
Details:
Order your angel to sap the mental strength of your target. The more
afflictions your target suffers from, the more this will drain(*).

* The drain scales to the number of afflictions they are suffering from.
Guilt, ablaze, spiritburn, and tenderskin increase the value by more
than other afflictions, followed by mental and limb based afflictions.
Inquisition increases more than anything else.
