Mace (Spirituality)                           Known: Yes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax:            SUMMON MACE
                   CALL MACE
                   SMITE <target> <limb> [CHASTEN <BODY|MIND|affliction>]
Works on/against:  Adventurers and denizens
Cooldown:          2.90 seconds of balance
Details:
Using your inherent link to the spirit world, you may summon a spiritual mace, with which to smite 
down your enemies. Note that your spiritual mace can be used with the targeting abilities in 
weaponry.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
