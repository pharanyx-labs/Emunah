5.8 BLEEDING
When you are hit by a cutting weapon in Achaea, you will usually begin to
bleed. After you begin bleeding, you'll continue to bleed health points every
so often. Your body will gradually clot the blood, but this will take time.

There are other methods available to quicken the clotting of blood. There are
certain class-related abilities, as well as an ability in survival that lets
you do it. There is also the moss tattoo, which, when inscribed on your body,
will automatically clot your blood for you.
